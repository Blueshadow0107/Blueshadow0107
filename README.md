## What I do

- **CFD and numerical methods:** Fortran and Python solvers verified against benchmarks, validated CFD in Fluent on HPC clusters (plus OpenFOAM and UCNS3D), mesh generation with Pointwise and Fluent Meshing.
- **Research:** my MSc thesis studied photosensitive reaction-diffusion media as physical neural networks, with a finite-difference solver verified by manufactured solutions (second-order convergence in 2D and 3D) and a 60+ experiment simulation campaign.
- **Machine learning:** PyTorch, surrogate modelling, fine-tuning small models for practical tools.
